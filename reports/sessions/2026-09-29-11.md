## 1. RNG stream mirroring (the big unlock)

The two drivers' streams used to sit at different positions (orig ~13.5 draws/tick of "pure seams" the port never consumed). Root causes found by stack-tracing orig draws and stubbing subsystems:

1. **`village.map.update` RNG skeleton** (village.js L2192-2214): per biome per tick, 1 faunaName draw ALWAYS + 1 spawn-chance draw while fauna is empty + 2 jitter draws on spawn (10% of biomes/tick). Ported as a draw-exact skeleton over 13 dumped biomes (`BiomeDef` in the world catalog). **JS falsy trap:** `faunaPenalty || 1.0` makes the village's 0 → 1.0 — the village biome DOES spawn.
2. **Five daily rolls** in `calendar.onNewDay` (L560-731): meteor base, unicorn pacifism, unicorn ironWill, rift, alicorn — all draw BEFORE their gates (`&&` order), so 5 draws/day even at fresh. Mirrored in the runner's OnNewDayTail (astro roll gated off — no library).

With both mirrored, per-tick draw counts match exactly (verified tick-by-tick via a draw-probe scenario: 57/50/43/37/41/33 identical over 12 ticks). Scenario `resop seed` is now only needed to align the PRE-existing divergence at scenario start (orig's 33 bootstrap draws).
