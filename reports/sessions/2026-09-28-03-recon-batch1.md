  claimed — first concrete discrepancy vs elmishKittens specs; feeds verify-01). Static
  defs are mutated in place.

  controllers, not science.js — the port needs that base controller shared.

  L451, weather L886, crypto walk L1152/1160) — deterministic under our patched stream,
