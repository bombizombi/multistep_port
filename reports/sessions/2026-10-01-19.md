## The space-08 root cause

The parked space-program scenario diverged at `resources/starchart/p`
(ORIG 0.001, FS 0). The long-standing "energy halving" hypothesis was wrong —
the probe (`oracle/wip/probe-sat2.cat`) showed orig buys **one** sattelite, not
two (science: 500000 − 100000 − 125000 − 175000 = 100000 exactly buys sattelite
#1; #2 needs 108000). The real bug: the FS catalog stores the *static def*
effects for space buildings — which are **all zeros**; orig's per-building
`calculateEffects` (the UI-render job) *wholesale-replaces* `self.effects` at
build time with the real values (`starchartPerTickBaseSpace: 0.001`, energy
production/consumption, storage maxes…).
